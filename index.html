📡 Garmin Data Privacy
Before we start
Your Garmin Venu 3 collects health data automatically. Here is what this dashboard uses, and how it is protected.

What Garmin tracks automatically

😴
Sleep — hours and sleep quality each night, used to track your recovery.
❤️
Resting heart rate — measured each morning, used as a fitness marker.
🚶
Daily steps — total movement each day, used to track conditioning.
⚡
Body Battery — Garmin's energy readiness score (0-100), calculated from your heart rate, sleep, and stress levels.

How your data is protected

🔒
Stored on this device only. No data is uploaded to any external server by this dashboard.
👤
Only you and your practitioner can see it. The dashboard is password-protected.
🗑️
You can withdraw at any time. Ask your practitioner to clear your data.
Legal basis: Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) — APP 3 (we only collect what your program needs), APP 8 (no overseas storage by this dashboard), APP 11 (password-protected access). Garmin Connect operates under its own privacy policy for data stored on their servers.
